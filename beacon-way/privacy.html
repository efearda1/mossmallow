Beacon Way — Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Mossmallow Studio · Last updated October 8, 2026 / Son güncelleme 8 Ekim 2026

English

Beacon Way asks for no account and does not collect personal data itself. There are no sign-ups or logins.

Your progress (story progress, collectibles, settings) is stored only on your device; deleting the game deletes it.

This game contains no ads, does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Full game (one-time)) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. "Restore purchases" is in Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
